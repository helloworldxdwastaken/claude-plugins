# Compare one room across camera shots

Use this procedure when a replacement belongs in an established recurring scene. Build the room authority from the owner's references and accepted earlier footage; for a reported mismatch against the beginning, open actual beginning frames. An image generated for the failing shot cannot establish that shot's room as correct.

## Establish camera families and landmarks

Save hashes and frame/time coordinates for accepted front, side and close views that actually exist. Identify the camera side/axis, height, viewing direction and subject/table orientation separately from the actor's gaze or head turn. A close view should agree with a crop or plausible view along the established camera axis; copying a portrait's setting does not establish the camera.

Record the visible spatial relationships that distinguish this set: wall planes/corners, panel pattern/order, doors/windows/curtains, lamps/candelabra, furniture, floor/rug, chair/table placement, light and reflections. Name what is visible, occluded or outside the crop. Do not infer an unseen wall or invent a floor plan. Perspective changes may move projected positions, but cannot create sofas, remove visible fixtures, mirror the set, or move a door to another wall.

## Inspect the actual new take

Make a side-by-side sheet with the accepted earlier room, corresponding original performance and the new candidate. Include neighboring accepted shots and the new shot's first/middle/last frames. Inspect native crops of background details as well as the whole composition. Trace landmarks across each cut and through camera movement; check that subject, chair and table have the expected angle in the same room. Check the actual delivered High/Saver/composited view when that is what is being approved.

Evaluate room geometry, camera perspective, subject orientation and framing separately. A take can improve the face and still fail the room. Matching cream/gold/green colors alone is insufficient. A correct audio duration or overall frame count cannot certify the visual camera sequence; measure actual picture cuts and motion independently.

For AI casino, compare the replacement against the established room in the beginning of the selected table video. Ornate panels, standing lamps, candelabra and curtains must retain their visible relationships. A newly invented blank corner or sofa arrangement is a mismatch unless the owner explicitly changes the set. Use current footage for actual facts; this example is not a universal room specification.

## Handoff and recheck

Mark an unexplained room landmark, axis or layout change **mismatch/hold**. Do not approve a paid expansion, asset admission or an overall match from a face-only check, a provider success status, or another reviewer's summary. Missing sufficient accepted views means room continuity is not assessed; say what evidence is missing. Do not demand pixel-identical backgrounds between genuinely different angles.

Specify the smallest correction: use the accepted camera-family guide, reconstruct a consistent background, or rerender the affected shot with the original performance and established room. Recheck the corrected take against the same frozen earlier anchors and both neighboring cuts. Record remaining uncertainty and cosmetic deviations; never rename a rejected new setting as an approved reference to make the repair pass.
